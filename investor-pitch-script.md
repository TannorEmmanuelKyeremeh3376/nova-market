# NovaMarket.com investor pitch script

## Slide 1 - The everyday upgrade

"Good morning. NovaMarket.com is a design-led commerce brand for people who want better everyday objects without spending hours searching for them. We curate useful goods across electronics, fashion, accessories and home, then make the buying experience feel as considered as the products."

## Slide 2 - The problem

"Online shopping has a discovery problem. Large marketplaces offer too much choice, inconsistent quality and little confidence. Independent brands often have great products but weak merchandising, fragmented checkout and no clear reason for a customer to return. The result is a noisy path from inspiration to purchase."

## Slide 3 - The solution

"NovaMarket.com turns that noise into a point of view. Every item is selected around a simple promise: useful, beautiful and made for repeat use. Customers can search and filter a focused collection, save favorites, see the details that matter, check out securely and return to a personal order history."

## Slide 4 - Product experience

"The product is already built around the full customer journey. The storefront supports live search, category and price filters, product detail pages, variants, stock signals, a persistent cart and wishlist, promo codes, responsive checkout and account-aware order history. The experience works on desktop and mobile and can be deployed as a fast Vite application."

## Slide 5 - Business model

"Our core revenue model is direct product margin on curated goods. The same merchandising system can support higher-margin private-label products, limited drops, bundles and brand partnerships. Over time, first-party account and order behavior can improve curation, retention and campaign efficiency without turning the storefront into an ad marketplace."

## Slide 6 - Go-to-market

"We would start with a narrow editorial point of view and a small set of hero categories. Customer acquisition can combine short-form product storytelling, creator seeding, email drops and referral incentives. The first retention loop is simple: a customer saves an item, receives a useful update or new drop, returns to the same account and checks out faster."

## Slide 7 - Why now and why us

"Customers increasingly expect marketplace convenience and independent-brand taste at the same time. NovaMarket.com is designed for that intersection. The technology is intentionally lean: a fast storefront, persistent customer state, hosted authentication as we scale, and Stripe Checkout for secure payments without building a payment stack from scratch."

## Slide 8 - Milestones and metrics

"Our first operating milestones should be measured with discipline: conversion rate, average order value, repeat purchase rate, wishlist-to-purchase conversion, contribution margin and customer acquisition payback. We would use the next phase to validate the strongest category, establish repeatable acquisition and add real customer accounts, inventory operations and fulfillment visibility."

## Slide 9 - The ask

"We are raising [amount] to fund [inventory or supplier commitments], customer acquisition experiments, production-grade identity and fulfillment, and the small team required to turn curation into a repeatable commerce engine. We are not asking investors to fund another generic marketplace. We are building a sharper, more trusted way to shop for the things people use every day."

## Closing

"NovaMarket.com is a focused answer to a broad problem: people want fewer, better choices and a shopping experience that respects their attention. We have the product foundation in place. The next step is proving that a strong point of view can compound into repeat customers, healthier margins and a durable commerce brand."

## Presenter notes

Replace `[amount]` and any unverified milestone language with actual numbers before presenting. Do not claim revenue, traction, retention, customer counts or supplier commitments until they are measured and documented.
